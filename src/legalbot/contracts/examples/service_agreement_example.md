# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement is made effective as of February 1, 2025 by and between Harbor Logistics LLC (the "Client") and Summit Consulting Group, Inc. (the "Provider").

## Services

The Provider shall provide supply-chain optimization consulting, including process audits, software recommendations, and quarterly performance reviews, as further described in one or more Statements of Work executed by the parties.

## Fees and Payment

The Client shall pay the Provider USD 12,000 per month. The Provider shall invoice monthly, and payment is due net 30 days from the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

## Term and Termination

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for 24 months. Either party may terminate for convenience upon 30 days' written notice, or immediately for material breach that remains uncured after 15 days' notice.

## Independent Contractor

The Provider is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of the Client. The Provider is responsible for its own taxes and benefits.

## Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's confidential information and use it only to perform under this Agreement.

## Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

## Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
